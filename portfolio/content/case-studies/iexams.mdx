## Overview
iExam is a school examination management system under **IBNSULEMAN TECH LTD**, built for JAMB/WAEC/NECO exam preparation. It ships an AI tutor and offline capability so students can keep working without a stable connection, and is currently live across 37 admin accounts with 75+ course mappings.

## Problem
Exam prep in this market needs to work under real constraints:
- unreliable connectivity in many schools, so exam access can't depend on being online
- exam integrity and access control across students, admins, and institutions
- content coverage broad enough to matter for JAMB/WAEC/NECO, without becoming unmaintainable

## What I Built
### Offline-first exam engine
- exam delivery designed to keep working without a live connection
- sync behavior that reconciles state once connectivity returns

### AI tutor
- integrated tutoring support layered on top of the exam content
- built to help students beyond just taking practice exams

### Security-first design
- stronger access boundaries between student, admin, and institutional roles
- safe handling of exam content and results
- reduced risk of common web vulnerabilities (secure defaults)

### Backend & operations (Django/DRF, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker)
- data modeling for courses, exams, and results across institutions
- production deployment and hosting maintenance
