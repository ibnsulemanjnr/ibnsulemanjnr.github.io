## Overview
iExams is an online exam platform under **IBNSULEMAN TECH LTD**. After an initial trial launch, I paused public rollout to upgrade the system to **modern security, scalability, and UX standards**. The relaunch build is currently **~60% complete**.

## Why we paused after trial launch
Trial launch validated the idea, but production relaunch needs:
- stronger security guarantees around exam integrity and user access
- more robust architecture for scale and reliability
- upgraded UI/UX to current expectations

## What’s being rebuilt / improved
### Platform modernization
- improved flows for students and admins
- clearer structure for exam creation, scheduling, and result handling
- readiness for production-quality rollout

### Security-first design
- stronger access boundaries between roles
- safer handling of sensitive exam content
- reduced risk of common web vulnerabilities (secure defaults)

### Scalability and reliability
- architecture choices that support growth
- operational readiness (deployability, configuration hygiene)
