## Overview
Gaskiya is a civic accountability platform under **IBNSULEMAN TECH LTD**, currently in development ahead of a Q4 2026 launch. It's built around incident reporting, project tracking, and a social feed, so communities can report issues and follow whether they get addressed.

## Problem
Civic accountability in most communities is fragmented:
- issues get reported informally (word of mouth, one-off complaints) with no durable record
- there's no shared, trackable view of whether a reported project or incident actually gets resolved
- residents have no lightweight way to follow progress the way they would follow a social feed

## What's Being Built
### Incident reporting
- structured reporting flow for civic issues, designed to be simple enough for broad use

### Project tracking
- a trackable record connecting a reported issue to its resolution status over time

### Social feed
- a feed model so accountability data is visible and followable, not buried in a form submission
